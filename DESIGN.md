# Café Jurídico Design System

## Core Aesthetic
- **Vibe:** "Harvard meets Pinterest aesthetic coffee shop." Elegant, youthful but professional, warm, premium.
- **Visuals:** Cinematic lighting, blurred coffee shop backgrounds, soft glassmorphism effects, warm overlays, subtle particles (dust/coffee/smoke).
- **Colors:** Deep coffee tones (`#2C1E16`), warm beige/cream (`#F5E6D3`), matte black (`#1A1614`), and soft gold (`#C5A059`).
- **Shapes:** Modern rounded corners (e.g., 20px-24px for cards), smooth edges, soft luxurious shadows.

## Typography
- **Headings:** Bold, elegant, modern.
- **Body:** Clean, legible, minimalist sans-serif.

## Components & Effects
- **Cards:** Use soft glassmorphism (translucent backgrounds with blur), soft shadows.
- **Buttons:** Bright, glowing or elegant styling with smooth hover transitions.
- **Animations:** Fade ins, floating elements, slow parallax, micro-interactions, smooth scroll. 
- **Layout:** Cinematic, mobile-first responsive, immersive full-screen sections.
